# Room Dashboard

A fun little project I built for a dedicated screen in my room.

The idea was to have a clean, ambient display running throughout the day that gives me a quick look at daily essentials without having to pick up my phone or open a bunch of apps. It brings together things like the time, local weather, what is on my schedule, tasks for the day, and other quick updates I like keeping an eye on.

Nothing complicated—just a handy personal display sitting in my room to keep my day organized at a glance.
